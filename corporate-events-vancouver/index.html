Rendezvous Collective / Corporate Events

CORPORATE EVENTS · VANCOUVER

Corporate events with hospitality at the centre.

For companies, executive assistants, internal event owners and teams who need a thoughtful partner for dinners, client gatherings and moments that matter.

Company dinners, client moments and executive gatherings.

A strong corporate event should feel intentional without asking the host to carry every moving piece. Rendezvous Collective supports gatherings where hospitality, logistics and relationship-building need to work together — from intimate leadership dinners and client receptions to team celebrations and selected multi-day programs.

Based in Vancouver and available beyond British Columbia, RDV approaches corporate events with the guest experience in mind and the operational details firmly underneath it.

What RDV can support.

  • Venue and hotel research
  • Private dining and restaurant sourcing
  • Vendor and partner coordination
  • Timelines, run of show and logistics
  • Food, beverage and guest experience
  • Onsite setup and event execution

Every brief is different. The role can be focused — finding the right room for an executive dinner, for example — or broader, with RDV coordinating partners and the event flow from planning through onsite delivery.

Designed for the people attending.

Corporate events are ultimately relationship environments. The best venue, menu and production choices are the ones that support the reason people are gathering: a conversation, a milestone, a client relationship, a leadership decision or simply time together away from the everyday.

That is why RDV looks at the full experience rather than treating venue sourcing, food and beverage, timing and guest flow as separate tasks.
