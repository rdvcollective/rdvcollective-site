Our story

A name rooted in hospitality.

Rendezvous takes its name from my grandfather’s diner, Diners Rendezvous — known to most simply as Rendezvous — where “Dinner Under the Stars” was the tagline. A restaurateur and a pillar of his community, he created more than a place to eat; he created a place where people felt known, returned often and became part of something. Everyone in town knew his name.

Rendezvous Collective carries that spirit forward: finding places worth remembering, creating reasons to gather, and noticing the tiny details that make an experience feel special. It is about curiosity, gratitude, beautiful moments and the elegance found in everyday life — at home, at an event or somewhere new in the world.

Gather well. Notice more. Remember longer.
