OUR STORY

A legacy of gathering.

Rendezvous is an ode to my grandfather, whose restaurant, Diners’ Rendezvous, brought people together through great food, warm camaraderie and genuine connection. It was a place where people felt welcome, known and part of something. RDV carries that spirit forward through thoughtful gatherings, memorable places and the details that make life feel richer.

Gather well. Notice more. Remember longer.
