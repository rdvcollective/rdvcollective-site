Rendezvous Collective / Weddings

WEDDINGS · VANCOUVER

Celebrations that feel personal, considered and present.

For couples who care about how the day feels as much as how it looks — with thoughtful planning underneath and room to actually experience it.

A wedding should feel like the people getting married.

Rendezvous Collective approaches weddings through hospitality: how guests arrive, where they gather, what they experience, how the room feels and whether the couple has the space to be present instead of managing the day.

RDV supports Vancouver-area celebrations and selected destination weddings with practical planning, creative production and hands-on onsite care.

Planning, coordination and the details between.

  • Venue and vendor research
  • Planning timelines and run of show
  • Guest flow and hospitality details
  • Creative production and custom signage
  • Partner and floral-design collaboration
  • Onsite setup, coordination and takedown

The goal is not to force every celebration into the same package. It is to identify where support will make the biggest difference and build a plan around the couple, the venue and the experience they want to create.

Elegance lives in the small things.

Thoughtful celebrations are often remembered through tiny moments: the way a table is lit, a handwritten detail, the timing of dinner, a welcome that feels warm, or a room that gives everyone a reason to stay a little longer.

Those details are where RDV's event operations and creative eye meet.
